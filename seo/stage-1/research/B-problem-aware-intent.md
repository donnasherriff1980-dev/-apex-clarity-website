# UK SERP research: problem-aware / "how to" clusters for Kenvio

**Method and limitations (read first).** All findings come from the WebSearch tool only (extended mode for each primary query, standard for variants, "UK" appended where helpful). No paid tools; no search volumes are given or implied. The tool returns organic result lists and snippets, not a rendered Google SERP, so I could NOT directly observe People Also Ask boxes, AI Overviews, video carousels or featured snippets. Where I list "likely PAA-style questions" they are inferred from the question-form titles and sub-topics that recur across ranking pages, and are labelled as inferred, not observed. The result set is a US-indexed engine approximating a UK SERP, so true UK rankings will differ somewhat; domain mix and content types are the reliable signal, exact positions are not. The last three supplementary checks (subcontractor insurance-expiry software, PAS 2035 evidence, Building Safety Act golden-thread for SMEs) did not run because the session search budget was exhausted; nothing in the 14 clusters depended on them.

## Cluster 1: How to manage contractor compliance
- **Primary:** how to manage contractor compliance (UK). **Variants:** contractor compliance checklist UK; managing subcontractor compliance construction UK.

- **Demand signal:** Moderate for the construction meaning; strong-but-polluted overall. Evidence: multiple dedicated UK guides (SafeContractor "ultimate 2026 checklist", CHAS, Pisys, SubComply, ContractorVault), accreditation bodies producing content, several micro-SaaS sites built specifically around the term (SubComply, ContractorComply, SubChecked, ContractorVault), Lexis+ checklist, university/estates contractor checklists. - **Top organic results:**

  3. pisys.co.uk (Dec 2025) — SaaS vendor blog; decent H&S framing (insurance, RAMS, training, inductions).
  4. cxcglobal.com — contractor-workforce vendor blog; IR35 angle.

- **Natural mapping to Kenvio commercial pages:** Clusters 1 + 8 → contractor/supply-chain compliance records; 2 + 10 → competence requirements & evidence with expiry; 3 + 4 → RAMS packs / method statements + document control; 5 (+9 as permit-to-load/unload) → permits/control of work with hand-back; 6 + 7 + 13 → governance overview, approvals queue, audit trail; 11 → toolbox talks with attendance; 12 → COSHH from hazard library; 14 → bulk import / audit trail / main product page. - **Where HSE/gov.uk dominates and the definitional query is unwinnable:** COSHH ("how to do a COSHH assessment", HSE basics/FAQ/examples), permit to work definition (HSE permits page + HSG250), temporary works definition (HSE page + FAQ + CITB template).
