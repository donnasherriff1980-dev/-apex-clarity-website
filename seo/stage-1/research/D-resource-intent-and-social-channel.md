# Research D: resource and template intent, plus organic social channel check

Method: 40 WebSearch calls (extended for primaries, standard for variants). The tool does not expose People Also Ask, AI Overviews or video carousels. No volumes given; demand inferred from SERP composition. The final domain-restricted verification batch (reddit.com, electriciansforums.net, community.screwfix.com, youtube.com, facebook.com, linkedin.com post-level, iosh.com, twforum.org.uk) did not run; those items are marked UNVERIFIED.

## Part 1: resource and template intent

### R1 RAMS checklist / RAMS template free UK / Word
- Demand: STRONG. Nine distinct UK vendors with "free RAMS template" pages; Etsy/Gumroad sellers; HandsHQ, HazardCo, HammerTech, PlanRadar, Constructionline explainers. "RAMS checklist" skews to explainers; no true checklist ranks.
- Top organic: sitemate.com (trial gate); thesitebook.co.uk (account gate); peninsulagrouplimited.com (form gate); protecting.co.uk (free); sitesamurai.co.uk (6 free templates); safetystack.co.uk (198 templates, PDF); docgen/ramsai/tradeyuk AI generators; Word variant: Etsy (paid), Gumroad, SafetyCulture library, fluix.io, handshq.com rams-tool (trial gate).
- Authority: none; HSE publishes no RAMS template (the Irish HSA does).
- Opportunity: MEDIUM. A "RAMS review checklist for approvers" is the differentiated angle; feeds the RAMS page.

### R2 Risk assessment template (HSE / construction)
- HSE's own template page (June 2024) with .docx/.odt and six worked examples, none for construction; mirrored by councils and LEPs. Construction variant held by small UK template shops and paid generators (Anyrisks £29, HSEdocs £8.99), swiftRMS free, healthsafetyzone.
- Opportunity: LOW generic; MEDIUM construction-specific (5x5 matrix, CDM references, trade-task library), linking to HSE.

### R3 Permit to work template / checklist / hot works
- Demand: MODERATE to STRONG. Many new UK micro-SaaS pages built in 2025 to 2026 (PermitPad "HSG250-aligned", PermitDesk .docx pack, swiftRMS, Ebrora 14 templates, Duress Word no email, HammerTech hot works, Simply-Docs paid, Etsy). "Checklist" skews to PTW system audit checklists (hseblog, GoAudits, Lumiform, SafetyCulture).
- Authority: hse.gov.uk HSG250 (2005 text, petroleum focus) and the human-factors PTW page only when "HSE" is in the query; CITB PTW PDF adjacent; a Wikipedia immigration page and template-farm subdomains rank, a quality gap.
- Opportunity: HIGH. No HSE/CITB template; a hand-back-aware pack (general, hot works, isolation, confined space) plus a PTW system self-assessment checklist would be the best asset. Feeds Permits.

### R4 Competency / training / skills matrix template
- Demand: MODERATE generic HR (Florence, Factorial, AIHR, Peoplebox); construction-specific WEAK to MODERATE and under-served; the Excel-construction query returns scraper PDFs. Only Ebrora (Word, UK construction) and AG5 (US certs) are construction-specific; CSCS variant: morta.io, CompMatrx, a John F Hunt case study PDF.
- Authority: none (no HSE, CITB, CSCS, CPCS page).
- Opportunity: HIGH. Nobody owns "construction competency matrix UK" with CSCS/CPCS/IPAF/PASMA/NRSWA/ECS/Gas Safe columns and expiry logic. Feeds Competence. Seeds the spreadsheet-to-system story.

### R5 Contractor compliance / subcontractor pre-qualification checklist
- Demand: MODERATE. SafeContractor 2026 "ultimate UK checklist"; Complys, Superdocu, Phoenix Gray, Corp-Intel 2026 checklists; CIS-tax checklists pollute. Pre-qual variant: Sitemate (trial), AU/US templates, council policies, Contracts Finder attachments; no clean UK pre-qual template exists.
- Opportunity: HIGH for a UK subcontractor pre-qualification/onboarding checklist (SSIP/CHAS, insurance limits, CIS, right-to-work, CSCS, RAMS, competence evidence, expiry); MEDIUM for "contractor compliance checklist". Title around "subcontractor" and "onboarding" to avoid the CIS meaning.

### R6 Audit readiness / H&S audit checklist / ISO 45001
- Demand: MODERATE (H&S audit checklist UK), STRONG (ISO 45001, international), WEAK (exact "audit readiness" in the UK; North American EHS vendors rank).
- Top organic: SafetyCulture x2; GoAudits (account gate); highspeedtraining (2021 care-home PDF); veritas-consulting (2017); gmb.org.uk; yoursafetyadvisor.com; readiness variant: evotix, work-wallet, velappity, summithsih, corpathway, field1st/fieldpie; ISO 45001: SafetyCulture x3, Lumiform, GoAudits, fldata, Miratag, LRQA.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM. Frame as "evidence readiness" (which record proves which requirement, where it lives, who owns it) for SSIP renewal, principal-contractor audit and ISO 45001 internal audit. Feeds Audit readiness.

### R7 Temporary works register template / TWC checklist
- Demand: MODERATE, niche, professional, driven by BS 5975-1:2024.
- Top organic: citb.co.uk free register PDF v02.00 (Jan 2023) with completed example (authority, PDF not Excel); SafetyCulture/Mitti community register and audit checklist; twforum.org.uk sample procedure; redfoxcc.co.uk (BS 5975:2019, superseded); ebrora.com class matrix (paid) and free approval trackers; mytemporary.works (paid forms); hbf.co.uk TW guidance (Jan 2025); TWC checklist variant: hse.gov.uk SIM operational guidance, LABC warranty PDF, Munich Re loss-prevention guide, BSI, recruiters; excel.datasheet-templates.com (mis-titled).
- Opportunity: MEDIUM-HIGH. Nobody offers a free structured Excel aligned to BS 5975-1:2024 with risk class, design-check category, permit-to-load/strike status and inspection dates; no TWC/TWS checklist exists. Link to CITB/TWf. Publish only when the module is confirmed.

### R8 Toolbox talk attendance sheet / register
- Demand: MODERATE, commoditised (HASpod, SafetyCulture, Sitemate, Insite, Trained Team, Maptrack; Scribd; a 2018 Australian contractor PDF; template-farm subdomains).
- Opportunity: LOW-MEDIUM; bundle only (attendance sheet + 12-month planner + register), not a standalone page.

### R9 COSHH assessment template / register
- Demand: STRONG (assessment) / MODERATE (register). Ten UK pages compete for the assessment (HS Direct gated, Duress, Complys 2026, Unibuild free, Paddl, SmartQHSE, HSEdocs, SafetyPapers, healthsafetyzone). Register variant: HASpod (account), SafetyCulture, NFB (members), Lumiform, Gridfox; no ready .xlsx surfaced.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM (assessment) / MEDIUM-HIGH (register with SDS link, storage, quantity, review date, linked assessment). Construction substance pack (cement, silica, solvents, isocyanates, lead, bitumen) differentiates. Feeds Risk assessments and COSHH.

### R10 Spreadsheet to system migration checklist / replacing spreadsheets
- Demand: NEGLIGIBLE to WEAK for the exact phrases; results are GRC/cybersecurity vendors (RegScale, Riskonnect, CoreStream, Hyperproof), one CMMS guide, one generic UK migration planner; only SiteSamurai and Buildern are UK construction and both self-promotional.
- Opportunity: LOW as search; MEDIUM as a sales-enablement asset (inventory trackers, clean, map fields, parallel run, read-only legacy, cutover sign-off) used in demos, LinkedIn and email. The searchable adjacent intent is the pain query ("training matrix spreadsheet out of date", "RAMS stored in email").

### R11 Compliance maturity / H&S maturity model / culture assessment
- Demand: WEAK for contractors; MODERATE for H&S practitioners generally (IOSH, Keil Centre SCMM, HSE Safety Climate Tool, paid, via Snap Surveys). "Compliance maturity assessment" is owned by cybersecurity/GRC (Hyperproof interactive, Anecdotes).
- Opportunity: MEDIUM as a lead-gen interactive asset, LOW as a search page. Avoid "safety culture" wording; use "compliance control maturity" or "operational control self-assessment".

### R12 Construction site induction checklist / template
- Demand: STRONG; nine UK pages (GoAudits, swiftRMS, AttendIQ, PIPTFW, Elec-Mate, L4 Labs, Tradehand, Maptrack, Popprobe) plus SafetyCulture/Lumiform/Sitemate libraries.
- Opportunity: LOW-MEDIUM; the asset does not map cleanly to a marketed module. Deprioritise.

### Cross-cluster patterns
- UK H&S template SERPs are dominated by small UK SaaS and consultancies, not HSE, CITB or trade bodies, except R2 (HSE template) and R7 (CITB register).
- SafetyCulture, Mitti, Lumiform, GoAudits and Sitemate appear in almost every cluster with community or generic templates; SafetyCulture labels its community templates as unverified. Beatable on UK specificity and quality.
- Scraper and template-farm domains rank for R4 and R3/R8, the clearest evidence of thin content.
- Gating is roughly 50/50; "no email required" is used as a ranking hook by several UK vendors (Duress, SafetyPapers, Harmny, PermitPad), so gated downloads would underperform.
- Priority for a low-authority specialist: R3 Permits (HIGH) > R4 Competency matrix (HIGH) > R5 Subcontractor pre-qual (HIGH) > R7 Temporary works Excel + TWC checklist (MED-HIGH) > R9 COSHH register (MED-HIGH) > R1 RAMS review checklist (MED) > R6 Audit evidence readiness (MED) > R2 construction RA (MED) > R11 maturity self-assessment (MED, non-search) > R8 toolbox (LOW-MED) > R12 induction (LOW-MED) > R10 migration (LOW as SEO).

## Part 2: organic social channel check

Verified from search results:
- LinkedIn is the only channel where UK H&S and compliance SaaS and consultancies have a verifiable organic presence: HandsHQ (uk.linkedin.com/company/handshq, about 3.3k followers; RAMS Analytics posts, award shortlisting; acquired by HSI Aug 2025; IIRSM partnership); SafetyCulture (about 38k followers, global) and SHEQSY (about 1.4k, UK NHS content); a UK construction compliance vendor post using #CDM2015 #HealthAndSafety #ConstructionManagement; the "Health and Safety Professionals in the UK and Ireland" private LinkedIn group (RS UK & Ireland, late 2023; reported by hsmsearch.com), not construction-specific; IOSH company page; practitioner LinkedIn Pulse articles ranking in Google for "safety culture maturity model".
- Follower counts and engagement could not be verified live; hashtag volumes could not be measured.
- Reddit, Electricians Forums, Screwfix Community, Facebook groups and YouTube did not surface on any open query for RAMS, permit or competence discussion; every query returned vendor guides, HSA/CITB PDFs or job adverts. These communities are UNVERIFIED, not confirmed dead.
- No dedicated SHP Online discussion forum exists (news, newsletter, podcast, webinars, SHP4Jobs, Women in Health & Safety Forum events); IOSH forum threads did not surface.

Verdict: LinkedIn is the strongest verifiable organic B2B channel for this buyer set. Forum, Reddit and video layers should be checked in a follow-up before being relied on.
