# Research A: UK SERP landscape, direct software-intent clusters

Method: WebSearch only (standard and extended), "UK" appended to approximate a UK SERP; 41 searches. SERP features (ads, People Also Ask, AI Overviews) are not observable with this tool. No volumes are stated; demand signals are qualitative reads of competitive density and page types. Three low-priority variants did not run ("RAMS management software", "CHAS Alcumus Veriforce contractor compliance software UK", "construction compliance management software UK").

## Cluster 1: Contractor compliance software
- Primary: contractor compliance software UK. Variants: contractor compliance management software; operational compliance software for contractors UK.
- Intent: transactional / commercial investigation.
- Demand: MODERATE to STRONG (UK-specific). At least 9 dedicated UK SaaS pages (SiteConnect /gb/, Complys, Heresafe, Sync, ExpiryFlow, ACREDIX, Contractor-Compliance.co.uk, Semieta, Be-Safe); several 2025 to 2026 listicles (peoplemanagingpeople, Complys, Heresafe, SiteSamurai, be-safetech). Un-qualified variant flips to US products (VelocityEHS, Veriforce, Comply Flow).
- Top organic: peoplemanagingpeople.com listicle (HR skew); siteconnect.io/gb (cites CDM 2015, priced by FTE); complys.co.uk (from £15/mo); contractor-compliance.co.uk (IR35 tool, intent collision); acredix.cl/en (thin); expiryflow.co.uk; syncsoftware.uk; heresafe.com (from £550/mo).
- Authority/government: none on the UK primary.
- Weak pages: acredix.cl; contractor-compliance.co.uk (IR35 only); glossary and directory stubs.
- Opportunity: HIGH. Small UK vendors with modest authority; no HSE page; term split between IR35 and H&S meanings, so an unambiguous H&S/supply-chain page has a clear lane.
- Notes: secondary queries "subcontractor compliance software UK", "supply chain compliance software construction", "contractor pre-qualification software UK". Anchors: CDM 2015, Right to Work, CIS.

## Cluster 2: Contractor management software UK
- Variants: subcontractor management software UK; contractor onboarding software UK construction; contractor management system construction UK CDM 2015.
- Intent: commercial, split three ways (freelancer payroll: Deel, Transformify, Worksome; construction ERP: Procore, Buildertrend, Archdesk, Access COINS; site H&S contractor control: Evotix Assure, SiteConnect, Heresafe, Contractor Genie).
- Demand: STRONG but polluted; G-Cloud listings rank; Capterra/Software Advice UK category pages.
- Top organic: transformify.org; peoplemanagingpeople.com; G-Cloud (Evotix Assure); theaccessgroup.com construction; buildern.com; Wikipedia (contract management); siteconnect.io/gb; softwareadvice.co.uk.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM. Head term too ambiguous; realistic plays are qualified tails. The CDM 2015 variant SERP is guidance only (CIOB, Designing Buildings) with no vendor.
- Notes: a G-Cloud listing is a cheap authority signal.

## Cluster 3: Construction compliance software
- Variants: site compliance software construction UK.
- Demand: MODERATE. SafetyCulture has a category page; 6+ UK vendors with exact pages (Competency Cloud, Insite, Sync, Complys, Boxcore, Bolster). "Site compliance" variant dominated by one small product (The Site Book) across directories.
- Top organic: my-compliance.co.uk blog (old, duplicated across subdomains); safetyculture.com category; peoplemanagingpeople; competencycloud.co.uk/construction; bolstersystems.com; insite.co.uk; syncsoftware.uk; boxcore.com.
- Opportunity: HIGH. Only SafetyCulture has authority; nobody covers RAMS + permits + competence + documents + audit trail.
- Notes: "CDM compliance software", "principal contractor compliance software", "Building Safety Act compliance software for contractors" are open. Anchors: CDM 2015, Building Safety Act gateways, golden thread, CSCS.

## Cluster 4: Health and safety management software UK
- Variants: for contractors; EHS software UK small business; H&S software construction; best H&S software construction UK 2026.
- Demand: STRONG, mature, vendor-saturated (ecoPortal, Access, Crysp, MY Compliance, Croner BrightSafe, Safesmart, Safety Hangar, SoftwareWorld listicle; GetApp and Capterra UK categories). "For contractors" variant is weaker (stale blog, BrightHR sector page, case study).
- Opportunity: LOW on the head term; MEDIUM on "health and safety software for contractors / construction UK".
- Anchors: RIDDOR, ISO 45001, HSG65, CDM 2015. Secondary: SHEQ software UK, HSEQ software for contractors.

## Cluster 5: RAMS software
- Variants: method statement software UK; risk assessment software UK; RAMS app construction; digital RAMS software.
- Intent: transactional; the most clearly commercial UK SERP in the set.
- Demand: STRONG and UK-distinct. 9/9 primary results are UK; four "best RAMS software UK" listicles (HandsHQ, The Site Book, Evalu-8, SafetyStack); 2025 to 2026 AI entrants with price-led titles (RAMS AI, swiftRMS £49/mo, RAMS PRO, Fast RAMS, RapidRAMS). "Risk assessment software UK" is polluted by cyber-risk tools; "method statement software" weaker.
- Top organic: handshq.com best-rams-software (HSI-owned since Aug 2025); thesitebook.co.uk compare; evalu-8.com; ramsai.co.uk; swiftrms.co.uk; rapidrams.co.uk; ramspro.co.uk; safetystack.co.uk compare.
- Authority: none; no HSE page on any RAMS variant.
- Weak pages: HandsHQ covid toolbox page ranking for method statement software; glossary pages; Riskex PDF; acronym collisions (Siemens RAMS).
- Opportunity: MEDIUM on the head (crowded, low to mid authority); HIGH on governance tails (approval with recorded reasons, version control, link to permits and competence, audit trail). Lucy-never-approves is a defensible angle.
- Notes: secondary "RAMS approval software", "contractor RAMS submission", "RAMS review and sign-off", "construction phase plan software". Anchors: CDM 2015, ISO 45001, HSG65, "no mandated RAMS format".

## Cluster 6: Permit to work software
- Variants: electronic permit to work system UK; control of work software; digital permit to work construction UK; best permit to work software UK.
- Demand: MODERATE, skewed to process industry and FM. G-Cloud (Ultan £2 to £30/user/mo, EcoOnline ePermits); GetApp and Capterra UK permit directories conflate with licensing software. "Control of work software" is effectively a dead SERP. HSE HSG250 PDF ranks for the electronic variant.
- Top organic: G-Cloud PTW listing; pisys.co.uk; getapp.co.uk; onlineinduction.com; safetymint.com/uk; ehs.com; eleco.com ShireSystem; ihf.co.uk.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM to HIGH for "permit to work software for construction / contractors UK"; LOW generic. "Control of work" is positioning, not a keyword.
- Notes: secondary "hot works permit software UK", "permit to work app construction", "permit to work with hand-back", "isolation management software". Anchors: HSG250, HSWA 1974, MHSWR 1999, Work at Height Regs 2005.

## Cluster 7: Competence management software
- Variants: training matrix software UK; certification expiry tracking software; skills matrix software construction UK; workforce compliance software UK; competency management software construction CSCS expiry.
- Demand: MODERATE generic; WEAK to MODERATE construction-specific. 9 UK vendors on the primary (CACI, Competency Cloud, SkillStation, Zebsoft, Complete Competence) plus two 2026 listicles. "Training matrix software UK" is template-dominated (universities, CloudAssess) with two small products. "Certification expiry tracking" is US-dominated. "Skills matrix software construction UK" returned only ITJobsWatch pages: no relevant page exists. Trade press on NOCN's Building Safety Act competence platform (Jul 2026).
- Opportunity: HIGH for construction-specific tails; MEDIUM for the generic head.
- Notes: secondary "CSCS card expiry tracking software", "construction training matrix software", "Building Safety Act competence software", "operative competence records". Anchors: Building Safety Act, CSCS/CPCS/NPORS/EUSR, HSE training records guidance.

## Cluster 8: Construction document control software
- Variants: compliance document management software UK; document control software UK small business; evidence management software compliance audit UK.
- Demand: STRONG for document control (wrong meaning: drawings/CDE; Procore, Access, InEight, 4PS, Collabor8online, DocTech); WEAK for compliance-document variant (Complys, DocuLocker, RegDocs365); NEGLIGIBLE for "evidence management software" (police digital evidence).

## Cluster 9: Temporary works software
- Variants: temporary works management software; temporary works coordinator software app.
- Demand: WEAK but specific and unserved. Three genuine products (City Temporary Works i-works portal, temporaryworks.online, TempTag Lite), a 2019-standard free template (rfxworks), paid packs (Structassure), Zutec/innDex/EcoOnline pages. Four of nine primary results were temporary recruitment software; the coordinator variant was 100% job adverts. No directory categories, no listicles.
- Authority: BSI (BS 5975-1:2024 product page), ukstandards.org.uk NOS. No HSE page observed here (see research B).
- Opportunity: HIGH on relevance-to-competition ratio; small absolute demand. Position under permits and control of work until the module is confirmed.
- Notes: secondary "temporary works permit to load software", "BS 5975 register template", "temporary works coordinator app". Anchors: BS 5975:2024 (2019 legacy), Work at Height Regs 2005, CDM 2015.

## Cluster 10: Toolbox talk app / software UK
- Demand: MODERATE, US-weighted and commoditised. SafetyCulture and Mitti duplicate "Top 9 of 2026" listicles; Sitemate /uk/; BIS Safety .com and .uk; Raken, Fulcrum, HCSS (US); a 2015 free iOS app still ranks. No UK listicle from a UK vendor.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM as a supporting page ("toolbox talk attendance record app UK"); nobody owns the CDM 2015 regulation 14 worker-engagement angle.

## Cluster 11: Site inspection software UK / inspection and action tracking
- Demand: STRONG but owned by field-ops platforms (Sitemate /uk/, Fieldwire /uk/, Novade /uk/ free tier, Procore en-gb, Insite, Capterra UK inspection category, Mitti listicle). "Action tracking software health and safety UK" is a real smaller niche (Notify, EcoOnline corrective action, Civica eSAFETY, Jacobs Track Record Safety on G-Cloud, Featherstone Safety Hub).
- Opportunity: LOW for "site inspection software"; MEDIUM for "H&S action tracking / corrective action tracking construction".

## Cluster 12: Operational compliance / operational control software
- Demand: NEGLIGIBLE as a search term. The UK-suffixed query resolved to Cluster 1's SERP; the exact phrase is owned by Sphera (enterprise EHS), Glide, Ruleguard, Quickbase; the "for contractors" variant returned AVEVA, Management Controls, OARS360, BuildOps, HCSS, Epsilon3.
- Opportunity: NONE as a keyword; fine as positioning language. Quickbase's definition ("day-to-day execution of safety, environmental and quality standards in the field" versus GRC) is a usable on-page explanation.

## Cross-cluster observations
1. The same small UK vendor set recurs across clusters 1, 3 and 12 (Complys, Sync, SiteConnect, Heresafe, Be-Safe, SiteSamurai, Competency Cloud, Insite, Semieta): low to mid authority, 2025 to 2026 content, none covering Kenvio's full span. Treat clusters 1 and 3 as one keyword family with two landing pages and shared internal links.
2. CHAS, Alcumus SafeContractor, Veriforce, Constructionline and Avetta were essentially absent from UK compliance-software SERPs.
3. UK demand is clearly real and distinct in RAMS software (strongest), contractor compliance software UK, health and safety management software UK and site inspection software. The RAMS SERP is an AI-generator price war; differentiate on governance.
4. Thin or unserved SERPs: temporary works register software; skills and training matrix software for construction; permit to work software for construction/contractors; action tracking H&S UK; CDM 2015 contractor management (guidance only).
5. Do not chase head terms owned by large field-ops/ERP platforms: site inspection, construction document control (drawings), health and safety management software head.
6. Merges: cluster 12 into 1; "evidence management" out of 8; "inspection and action tracking" into an H&S action-tracking sub-cluster; "control of work software" as positioning inside 6.
7. Regulatory anchors recurring: CDM 2015; Building Safety Act and golden thread; BS 5975:2024; HSG250; ISO 45001 and HSG65; CSCS/CPCS/NPORS/EUSR and CITB; RIDDOR; ISO 19650. PAS 2035 did not appear in any SERP observed.
8. G-Cloud Digital Marketplace listings rank on page one for many of these queries.
