# Research C: UK SERP landscape, industry-specific (A) and alternative/comparison (B) clusters

Method: 44 WebSearch queries (extended for primaries, standard for variants). Ads, People Also Ask and AI Overviews are not observable with this tool. No volumes given; demand inferred from SERP composition. A reliable thin-query tell: Capterra/GetApp foreign-TTLD pages for one product ranking for a UK query.

## Part A: industry-specific

### A1 Electrical contractors
- Primary: compliance software for electrical contractors UK. Variants: software for electrical contractors UK; electrical contractor management software; NICEIC compliance software.
- SERP reads "compliance" as certification (EICR, EIC, Part P) and job management. Demand: STRONG for job management/certificates; MODERATE "compliance"-qualified; WEAK for NICEIC compliance software (Clik listings across country TLDs).
- Top organic: GetApp electrical-contractor category; propellerpowered.co.uk; commusoft.co.uk; klipboard.com; complys.co.uk; bolstersystems.com (building-owner side); itrade.net listicle; field-ascend.com; tradestackhq.com listicle. Variants: joblogic.com "7 tools compared"; Capterra/GetApp UK directories; Powered Now, ServiceM8, Simpro, Tradify named.
- Trade bodies: NICEIC (certificate check); ECA only via eRAMS (free RAMS tool for members, built with CHAS; £100 to £375/yr non-members). NAPIT, SELECT, JIB absent.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM. Nobody owns H&S/operational compliance for electricians, but a page must say "not certification software; sits alongside your cert tool".
- Anchors: BS 7671:2018+A2:2022, EICR/EIC, Part P, Electricity at Work Regulations 1989, scheme requirements, JIB/ECS cards, CDM 2015.

### A2 M&E / building services

- Demand: WEAK to MODERATE; compliance intent unproven. Trade bodies (BESA, ECA, CIBSE, BSRIA) absent.
- Opportunity: MEDIUM-LOW; combine with A1 into one "Electrical and M&E contractors" page.
- Anchors: BS 7671, Gas Safe, F-Gas/REFCOM, SFG20, CIBSE commissioning codes, BESA TR/19, BSRIA guides, L8/HSG274, CDM 2015.

## Part B: alternative / comparison

### B1 RAMS software vs spreadsheets / templates / Word
- Demand WEAK to MODERATE. Exact pages: thesitebook.co.uk, sitesamurai.co.uk, sitemanagerai.com (Word alternative), velappity; padded with off-topic results. All comparison content is vendor-authored.
- Substantiable: YES (HSE prescribes no format; failure modes are version control, retrospective sign-off, no hazard library, distribution, retrieval, no link to permits/competence; concede cost of switching and low-volume suitability).
- Missing from listicles: an honest "when Word is fine" threshold, approval governance, linkage to permits and competence, offline reality, non-vendor sources. Opportunity: MEDIUM.

## Cross-cluster observations
1. Sectors by observable software-buying demand: construction/CDM; RAMS (category); FM (CAFM, client-side); electrical (job management/certification); renewables (design/MCS); principal-contractor subcontractor compliance (rising, fragmented); fire protection (field service); maintenance/social housing (client-side); PAS 2035 (lodgement); M&E (estimating); roofing; civils; scaffolding, utilities, telecoms, passive fire (negligible).
2. Build dedicated industry pages for three groups only: construction contractors and principal contractors (A4 + A11); electrical and M&E contractors (A1 + A2, with the "not certification software" disclaimer and eRAMS-plus positioning); FM and maintenance service providers (A3 + A5, contractor side).
3. Sections, not pages: fire protection; renewables scaling to commercial; social housing and retrofit scheme contractors; civils; roofing.
4. Ignore for search: scaffolding; utilities/telecoms; passive fire; PAS 2035 coordinator tooling; generic "health and safety software UK".
5. The RAMS SERP is a 2025 to 2026 AI-generator land-grab; only HandsHQ sells governance. Kenvio's differentiator is absent from every ranking page.
6. Comparison pages worth building, in order: HandsHQ alternative; best RAMS software UK (criteria-led); subcontractor compliance software comparison; RAMS in Word vs software; electronic PTW vs paper for contractors; best construction compliance software UK (disclosed).
7. Not worth building: other vendor alternatives; scheme "vs" pages; Smartsheet for RAMS; "contractor management software UK"; SafetyCulture head-to-head.
8. Trade bodies barely appear on any software query; partnerships (ECA, BESA, SELECT, ASFP, BAFE) are an off-SERP distribution route. ECA's free eRAMS is a substitute to position above.
9. Thin-SERP tells to exploit: foreign Capterra/GetApp TLDs (CDM compliance software, fire door inspection, Protecht); press releases and PDFs in the top ten; 2021 to 2024 pages still ranking. "CDM compliance software", "electronic permit to work for contractors" and "subcontractor compliance software" are the three thinnest commercially relevant SERPs found.
10. Every "best of" page encountered is vendor- or affiliate-authored; no independent UK comparison exists. Kenvio's own comparison will be discounted unless it concedes competitors' strengths and publishes verifiable, dated prices. SERP features should be checked manually in a UK browser before committing page briefs.
