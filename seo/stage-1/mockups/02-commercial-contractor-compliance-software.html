Why spreadsheets and folders fail

Compliance that lives in six places cannot be proven from one.

✕Onboarding approved once, then insurance and SSIP quietly expire mid-contract.
✕RAMS accepted by email. No record of which revision the operatives were briefed on.
✕Cards photographed into a shared drive. Expiry dates tracked from memory.
✕Audit request arrives. Two people spend a week rebuilding evidence that already existed.
What Kenvio does

One record per contractor, per job, per decision.

1

Onboarding requirements with approvals

Define what each contractor must provide. Each requirement is approved, waived with a reason, or outstanding, and the linked document carries its own expiry date.

2

RAMS review, approval and acknowledgement

Subcontractor RAMS move through draft, review and approval. Acknowledgements are recorded per person against the specific revision. How revisions are handled →

3

Competence evidence with expiry

Requirements per role, evidence per person, verification status and expiry. Gaps are listed to work through, not discovered on site.

4

Permits with hand-back

Issue, extend, suspend, cancel and hand back with a recorded reason and the completion state captured.

5

Inspections, findings and actions

Templated inspections raise findings that become owned, dated actions and are closed with verification notes.

6

Audit trail and governance overview

Every governed record carries who did what and when. The overview lists what is outstanding across every project. It is not a compliance score.

Frequently asked

Questions buyers ask on this page

Is this for managing our subcontractors, or our own compliance?

Both. A principal contractor uses it to onboard and govern the supply chain. A subcontractor uses the same records to evidence its own RAMS, competence and permits to the contractors it works for.

Does Kenvio certify that we are compliant?

No. Kenvio holds and governs the evidence. The duty holder remains responsible under the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, and your competent people make every approval decision.

What does Lucy do?

Lucy drafts first versions, retrieves records with their sources and points out gaps. She does not approve, certify or authorise anything.

Can we import what we already have?

Yes. Document control includes bulk import so an existing back catalogue is brought in rather than re-created.

How is it priced?

Per organisation, per month: Core £299, Growth £599, Business £999, Enterprise scoped. All + VAT. Tier fit is agreed before you commit.
