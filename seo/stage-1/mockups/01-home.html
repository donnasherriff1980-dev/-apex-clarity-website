The problem

The work gets done. The evidence goes missing.

Contractors rarely lose a contract because the work was unsafe. They lose it because they could not prove, on the day they were asked, who controlled it.

✕RAMS revised on Tuesday. The crew is still working to Friday's version, and nobody can show who read which.
✕Permit extended by phone. Hand-back never recorded. The area's state is anyone's guess.
✕A card expired six weeks ago. It is found during the client's audit, not before.
✕Inspection raised a finding. The action lives in a WhatsApp thread and was never closed.
✕Subcontractor insurance lapsed mid-contract. The onboarding folder said "approved" in March.
✕Temporary works loaded before the check was signed. There was a register, in a spreadsheet, on one laptop.
