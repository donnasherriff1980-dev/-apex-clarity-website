Qualification and demo CTA · proposed treatment

One short qualification, three places it appears.

The same four-step flow replaces the current single long contact form. It is short enough to complete on a phone, specific enough to route the enquiry, and never asks for anything the sales call would not need.

Design notes
  1. A Inline block at the end of every commercial and guide page. Email and company first, so a half-completed form still creates a usable prospect.
  2. B Step 2 and 3 use tap-to-select chips rather than dropdowns. Sector, size, active projects and current tooling are the fields Platform Admin prospects already model (sector, priority, source).
  3. C Final step offers a time slot or "call me". The confirmation says what happens next and repeats the honest boundaries (no certification, Lucy does not approve).
  4. D Floating button appears after the first screen, labelled by page context, and never over the cookie banner.
  5. E Founder Pilot is shown as a single line on the pricing and demo pages only, with the full terms reserved for the call. Standard prices are shown unchanged.
